Picture this: 10,000 strangers, all moving at high speed, in metal boxes, through the same narrow ribbons of tarmac. No rules. No signals. No order.

<MediaHint
  type="diagram"
  alt="Two roads: one chaotic with cars going every direction, one orderly with clear lanes and signals"
  caption="Without rules — chaos. With rules — flow."
  placement="inline"
/>

That chaos is exactly what road rules prevent. In Kenya, two documents govern everything that happens on the road: the **Traffic Act** and the **Highway Code**.

<Callout type="analogy">
  Think of the Traffic Act as the constitution — the law itself. And the Highway Code as the practical guidebook — how to actually behave on the road, explained in plain language.
</Callout>

They work together. One sets the rules; the other teaches you how to follow them.
