**Defensive driving** is the discipline of assuming that every other road user is about to make a mistake, and arranging your speed and space so their mistake cannot become your crash.

It is not paranoia. It is a planning mindset. The matatu will cut in. The boda boda will overtake on the left. The pedestrian will step out without looking. You are not surprised, because you have already left room for it.

<Callout type="analogy">
  A goalkeeper does not stand still and hope no one shoots. They watch the hips,
  anticipate the shot, and start moving before the ball does. Defensive driving
  is the goalkeeper's posture, applied to every kilometre.
</Callout>

The principles below are the ones the NTSA examiners will ask about, and they are the same ones that explain why long-distance Kenyan truck drivers, who do millions of road kilometres, have far lower crash rates than weekend drivers.
