Stay in gear. Brake early, brake gently, change down before the bend, not on it.

<Rule>
  Engine connected, always. Neutral is for stopped. Anything else is gambling
  with two brakes and using one.
</Rule>

The freewheeling habit usually hides behind the words _I am just trying to save fuel_. In a fuel-injected car built this century, you save nothing and risk everything. Spend the shilling. Keep the gear.

Next we widen the lens. Stopping distance is one slice of a bigger discipline: managing the space all around your car.
