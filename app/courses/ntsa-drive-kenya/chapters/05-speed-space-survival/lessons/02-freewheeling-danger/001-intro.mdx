**Freewheeling** is driving with the clutch pressed in or the gear stick parked in neutral, letting the car roll under its own momentum. No engine pulling, no engine pushing back. Just a heavy metal box and gravity.

It feels efficient. It feels smooth. A whole generation of matatu drivers swore it saved fuel down the long descent into Naivasha. It does not. And it is one of the most quietly dangerous habits a Kenyan driver can pick up.

<Callout type="analogy">
  A connected engine is like holding a friend's hand on a steep staircase. You
  can push, you can pull, you can steady each other. Freewheeling is letting go
  at the top step and hoping the railing catches you.
</Callout>

In the next block you will meet the four specific things you lose the moment you take the engine out of the loop.
