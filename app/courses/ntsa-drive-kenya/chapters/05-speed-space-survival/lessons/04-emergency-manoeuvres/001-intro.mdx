In a real emergency, you will not have time to think. You will have time to recall. Whatever your hands and feet have rehearsed is what they will do.

This lesson loads four rehearsals: brake failure, tyre blowout, a wheel dropping off the tarmac, and headlights dying at night. Each one has a counter-intuitive first step that goes against panic, which is exactly why you need to drill it now, in calm, before the road tests you.

<Callout type="rule">
  Panic says: brake harder, swerve sharper, hit the lights. The correct answer
  is almost always the opposite: lift off, hold straight, look where you want to
  go.
</Callout>

Flip every card. The four moves below have probably saved more Kenyan lives than any single piece of legislation.
