Space is the resource you spend on every decision. The driver with the bigger gap has the bigger menu of options.

<Rule>
  Match your speed to the worst part of your space envelope. If one side is
  closed, the whole car is in a closed road.
</Rule>

<Callout type="tip">
  Open, closed, changing. Run those three words through your head every time you
  crest a hill, leave a town, or join a new road. It is the cheapest mental
  habit a Kenyan driver can build, and the one the NTSA exam quietly tests in
  every defensive-driving question.
</Callout>

Sometimes, despite all your spacing, something fails. Tyre, brake, lights, road edge. That is the next lesson: when the plan breaks, what do you do in the first two seconds?
