You have finished the course.

Eight chapters, forty-something lessons, and hundreds of small decisions practised until they became instincts. Signs read at a glance. Roundabouts handled with confidence. Stopping distances felt, not calculated. Emergencies rehearsed before they happen.

<Rule>
  Your licence is a starting line, not a finish line. Every safe trip you take
  from here is practice for the next one.
</Rule>

The road will keep teaching you things this course never could: how a particular junction in your neighbourhood behaves at 6pm, how your specific car responds in heavy rain, how to read a boda boda's intent from twenty metres away. Pay attention to those lessons too.

<Callout type="tip">
  The day you stop learning is the day you become dangerous. Re-take the
  simulated exam every six months for the first two years of driving. The habits
  stay sharp, and the marks always show up where you have gone soft.
</Callout>

Drive safely, drive courteously, drive home every time. Good luck on exam day. You are ready.
