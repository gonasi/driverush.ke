Theory comes first. You will not touch a steering wheel until you have passed it.

<Rule>
  NTSA theory exam: 40 multiple-choice questions, 30 minutes, 80% pass mark. You
  may miss 8 questions, not 9.
</Rule>

The format is computer-based at most NTSA centres. You log in with your reference number, the timer starts when you click Begin, and the system locks at 30 minutes. You see your score on screen the moment you submit.

Bring with you: your original ID, your medical certificate, your eyesight test result, and your booking confirmation. Arrive at least 30 minutes early. Phones are surrendered at the desk, so do any last-minute revision in the parking lot, not in the exam room.

<Callout type="tip">
  The questions are drawn from the Highway Code and the Traffic Act, in
  proportions of roughly 60/40. Sign-recognition questions are usually 8 to 10
  of the 40. Knowing them cold is the easiest mark-bank on the paper.
</Callout>

Pass theory and you book practical. Fail and you rebook theory. There is no shortcut.
