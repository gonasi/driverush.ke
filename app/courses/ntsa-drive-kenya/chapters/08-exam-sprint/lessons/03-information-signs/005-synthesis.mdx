Three sign families, three shapes, three jobs.

<Mnemonic>
  Circle commands, triangle warns, rectangle informs. Border colour fine-tunes
  the meaning.
</Mnemonic>

Combine that with the blackout rule, and you have the spine of every sign-related question on the NTSA theory paper.

<Callout type="tip">
  When a question shows two signs together (for example, a warning triangle plus
  a regulatory speed limit), the regulatory sign always wins on priority.
  Warnings prepare you. Regulations bind you.
</Callout>

Three lessons in. Two more drills before the simulated exam: customer care and work planning, then the day itself.
