Information and guidance signs do not order or warn. They help.

<Rule>
  Rectangles inform. They tell you where things are: a hospital, a petrol
  station, a parking zone, the start of a motorway. You read them, you decide.
</Rule>

The exam tests two things here. First, can you identify the symbol. Second, do you know how to behave in special zones: hospital silence, school crossings, motorway entry rules.

<Callout type="analogy">
  A regulatory sign is your boss telling you what to do. A warning sign is a
  friend tapping your shoulder. An information sign is the map on the wall:
  useful, neutral, and silent unless you read it.
</Callout>

Special class of guidance signs deserves its own treatment: the ones that route you through a junction. Those are next.
