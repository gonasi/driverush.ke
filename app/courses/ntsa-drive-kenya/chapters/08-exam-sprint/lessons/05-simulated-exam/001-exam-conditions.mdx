This is the dress rehearsal.

The real NTSA theory exam puts 40 multiple-choice questions in front of you. You have 30 minutes. The pass mark is 80%, which means you can afford to miss 8 questions, no more.

<Rule>
  Treat this simulation exactly like the real thing. Phone away. Quiet space.
  Timer running. No going back to look up answers.
</Rule>

The simulation that follows is a shortened, weighted version covering every chapter you have studied: signs, road lines, roundabouts, parking, alcohol and fatigue, MSM, stopping distance, emergencies, and customer care. Hit 80% here and you are ready.

<Callout type="tip">
  If a question stumps you, eliminate the two obviously wrong options first,
  then pick the safer of the remaining two. The NTSA almost never marks the
  cautious answer wrong.
</Callout>

Take a deep breath. Then start.
