That was your dress rehearsal. Whatever your score, the next 60 seconds matter more than the exam itself.

<Rule>
  Above 80%: you are exam-ready. Between 60% and 80%: review your weak chapters
  before booking the test. Below 60%: go back and replay the chapters that hurt
  you.
</Rule>

If you missed any sign questions, return to lessons 8.1 to 8.3 and run the flash and memory games until you can name every sign on instinct. If you missed any speed, stopping-distance, or roundabout questions, replay Chapters 1 and 5. Alcohol, fatigue, or first-aid misses: Chapters 2 and 7.

<Callout type="tip">
  The biggest predictor of passing the real NTSA exam is not raw knowledge, it
  is calm. Practise this simulation twice. The second time, you will be
  measuring nerves as much as memory, and that is the point.
</Callout>

One more lesson to go. The exam day itself: what to bring, what to expect, and how to walk out with a licence in your hand.
