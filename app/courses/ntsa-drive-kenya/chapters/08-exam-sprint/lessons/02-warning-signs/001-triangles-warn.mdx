If regulatory signs are the officer's hand, warning signs are the officer's voice from 100 metres away: something is coming, get ready.

<Rule>
  Triangles warn. A red border points upward, the symbol inside tells you what
  is coming. You do not stop on a warning. You prepare.
</Rule>

Preparing usually means three things in sequence: ease off the accelerator, check your mirrors, and place your hands ready to steer or brake. By the time the hazard arrives, you have already adjusted.

<Callout type="analogy">
  A bend warning sign is a kettle whistle. The water is not boiling yet, but in
  a few seconds it will. You move the cup now, not when it spills.
</Callout>
