Warning signs are an early-warning system. Treat them as instructions to your future self, fifty metres further down the road.

<Rule>
  A warning sign always triggers three actions: ease off, mirror check, hands
  ready. Then the actual response to the hazard.
</Rule>

In the NTSA exam, warning-sign questions usually ask one of two things: what does this symbol mean, or what should you do when you see it. Knowing the symbol is half the mark. Naming the correct response wins the other half.

<Callout type="tip">
  When in doubt on a warning-sign question, the safest answer is almost always
  the one that involves reducing speed and preparing to stop. The NTSA never
  marks caution as wrong.
</Callout>
