One trick worth banking before you move on: when an exam question shows you a regulatory sign and you blank on the meaning, work the border first.

<Mnemonic>
  Red border bans. Blue body commands. No border at all? It is probably a
  warning or information sign in disguise, not regulatory.
</Mnemonic>

Knowing the family from the border gets you to two plausible answers out of four. From there, the diagonal slash (when present) confirms a prohibition, and an arrow confirms a direction order.

<Callout type="tip">
  In the real NTSA test you have roughly 45 seconds per question on average.
  Spend the first 5 seconds reading the border, not guessing the meaning. That
  habit alone lifts most scores by several marks.
</Callout>
