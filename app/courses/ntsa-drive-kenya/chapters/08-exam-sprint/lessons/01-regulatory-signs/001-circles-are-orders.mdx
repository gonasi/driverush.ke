Welcome to the sprint. The NTSA theory exam leans hard on signs, and regulatory signs are where most candidates lose easy marks. The good news: they all follow one shape rule.

<Rule>
  Circles give orders. If the circle has a red border, it is a prohibition (do
  not). If the circle is solid blue, it is mandatory (you must).
</Rule>

Stop and Give Way break the circle pattern with their own unique shapes, but they belong to the same family: orders you must obey.

<Callout type="analogy">
  Think of a regulatory sign as a traffic officer's hand. It does not warn you
  about something coming up. It commands you, right now, to do or not do a
  thing.
</Callout>

In this lesson you will drill every regulatory sign in the NTSA set until you can name them on instinct. Speed matters: real exam questions show one sign and four options, and you have seconds to decide.
