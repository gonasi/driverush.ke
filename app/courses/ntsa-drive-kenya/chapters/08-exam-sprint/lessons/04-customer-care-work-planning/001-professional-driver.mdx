The NTSA does not test your character. It tests whether you understand the obligations of a professional driver, because every licensed driver is one, whether you carry a fare or your own family.

<Rule>
  A professional driver plans the trip before starting the engine: route, fuel,
  rest stops, weather, vehicle check, passenger needs. The drive itself is the
  easy part.
</Rule>

Customer care is not just for matatu and taxi drivers. It means courtesy to everyone sharing the road, calm communication with passengers, and a tidy, road-legal vehicle.

<Callout type="analogy">
  Imagine you booked a flight and the pilot showed up tired, with no flight
  plan, in a dirty aircraft. You would walk off. Apply the same standard to
  yourself.
</Callout>

This lesson sits in the exam sprint because the NTSA asks these questions directly, often paired with hours-of-service maths.
