Customer care, hours-of-service, trip planning. Three pillars the NTSA examiners love to test, because they separate a hire from a hazard.

<Callout type="tip">
  When the question asks "what is the FIRST thing you do" in a customer-care or
  planning scenario, the answer is almost always the planning step that should
  have happened BEFORE driving. The exam rewards prevention over reaction.
</Callout>

You have one drill left before the simulated exam. Take a breath, then walk into the next lesson the way you would walk into a real NTSA test: focused, prepared, and not rushed.

<Rule>
  The simulated exam is your dress rehearsal. Treat it like the real thing.
  Phone away, timer on, no second guesses.
</Rule>
