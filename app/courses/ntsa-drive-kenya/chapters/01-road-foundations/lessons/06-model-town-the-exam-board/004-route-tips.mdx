Examiners watch two things on the model town: did you obey every sign in the correct order, and did you steer like you would on a real road — not like you're playing a game?

<Callout type="tip">
  Use your indicators on the model town. Use mirrors before every lane change.
  These habits cost zero marks to do and many marks to skip.
</Callout>
