The model town is a scaled-down road network at every NTSA centre. Every chapter-1 idea is built into it: a roundabout, a dual carriageway, two-way streets, a stop sign, a give-way junction, a pedestrian crossing, angle and flush parking, and a yellow kerb you must not stop on.

<Rule>
  Three routing rules to memorise: take the shortest legal route, obey every
  sign and signal exactly as on a real road, and never cut through parking or
  the wrong-way lane to save a turn.
</Rule>
