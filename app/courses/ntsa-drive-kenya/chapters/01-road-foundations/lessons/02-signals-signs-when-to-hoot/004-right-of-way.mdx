Some road users carry absolute priority. When you hear a siren or see flashing blue lights, you don't negotiate. You pull aside, slow or stop, and let them through.

<Mnemonic>
  Sirens and blues, motorcades, and a traffic officer's hand: those three trump
  every other rule on the road, including the lights.
</Mnemonic>

The order is simple: a traffic officer directing traffic overrides every other signal. Emergency vehicles with sirens or blue lights come next. A presidential motorcade follows the same rule. Everything else — including a green light at the junction — is paused until they pass.
