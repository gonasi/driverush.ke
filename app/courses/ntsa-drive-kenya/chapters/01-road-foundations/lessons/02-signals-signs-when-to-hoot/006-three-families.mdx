Every road sign in Kenya belongs to one of three families. The **shape and border** give the family away before you read a single word.

<Mnemonic>Triangles warn. Circles order. Rectangles inform.</Mnemonic>

Warning signs are triangles pointed up with a red border, they say "something is coming, get ready." Regulatory signs are circles: red borders for things you must **not** do, blue circles for things you **must** do. Information and guidance signs are rectangles in blue or green, they help you find your way but don't command you.

<Callout type="tip">
  If you don't know the meaning, you can still read the family. A triangle ahead
  always means "prepare." Slow your eyes, slow your foot, then read the picture.
</Callout>
