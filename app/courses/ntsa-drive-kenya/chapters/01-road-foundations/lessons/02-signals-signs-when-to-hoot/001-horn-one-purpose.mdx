The horn has exactly one legal use: **to warn another road user that you are there**. That's it. Not to scold, not to chat, not to announce arrivals at the gate.

<Rule>
  The horn is a warning device, used only while the vehicle is moving, and only
  to alert another road user to your presence.
</Rule>

<Callout type="warning">
  Hooting at a stationary car, leaning on it in traffic, or sounding it near a
  hospital or school is an offence. Police can and do stop drivers for it.
</Callout>
