Think of a Kenyan roundabout as a clock you read counter-clockwise. Your entry is 6 o'clock. Going **straight** brings you out at 12. **Right** is at 3. **Left** is at 9. **U-turn** is back to 6.

<Rule>
  Five laws of every roundabout: give way to your right, signal your intent
  before entering, stay in your lane, exit from the outermost lane (Lane 1), and
  signal off as you leave.
</Rule>

The lane you pick at entry decides what exits you can reach. Get the lane right and the rest is just steering.
