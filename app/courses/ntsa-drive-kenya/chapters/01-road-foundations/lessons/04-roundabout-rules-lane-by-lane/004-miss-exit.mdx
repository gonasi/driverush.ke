Missed your exit? **Stay calm. Don't swerve.** Go around once more.

<Rule>
  If you miss an exit, complete another lap. Never cut across lanes to grab an
  exit, it is the single most common cause of roundabout collisions in Nairobi.
</Rule>

A second lap costs you fifteen seconds. A cut-across costs you a fender, a fine, and possibly the other driver.
