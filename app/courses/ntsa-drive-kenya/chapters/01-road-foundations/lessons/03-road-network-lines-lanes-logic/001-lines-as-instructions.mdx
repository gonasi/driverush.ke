Road lines are silent instructions. Once you can read them, you'll know what every road allows or forbids without a single sign.

Two questions answer almost everything: what **colour** is the line, and is it **continuous** or **broken**? The answer tells you who you're separated from and whether you can cross.

<Callout type="analogy">
  Think of lines as fences. White fences separate friends going your way. Yellow
  fences separate strangers heading at you. A continuous fence is locked. A
  broken one has gates.
</Callout>
