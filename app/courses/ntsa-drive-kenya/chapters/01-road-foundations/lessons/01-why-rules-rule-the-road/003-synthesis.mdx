Now you've met both pillars of Kenya's road law.

<Rule>
  You are legally responsible for knowing both the Traffic Act and the Highway
  Code before you drive. "I didn't know" is not a defence.
</Rule>

Both documents are publicly available. Treat them the way you'd treat the rules of any game you're about to play, except the stakes here are real.

<Callout type="tip">
  In the NTSA theory exam, questions that reference rules and penalties are
  drawn from the Traffic Act. Questions about behaviour, courtesy, and safe
  technique come from the Highway Code. Knowing which document applies helps you
  understand *why* the answer is what it is.
</Callout>
