Two parking styles cover everything you'll meet on a Kenyan street. **Angle parking** has cars wedged into the kerb at roughly 45°, common in mall car parks and CBD bays. **Flush parking** (parallel) puts the car alongside the kerb, parallel to traffic, used on most public streets.

Each has its own rule for entry, exit, and where you may park at all. Knowing which is which keeps your bumper, your licence, and your wallet intact.
