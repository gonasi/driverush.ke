<Mnemonic>
  Angle in nose-first. Flush in reverse-first. Yellow says go. White says stay.
</Mnemonic>

The reverse-in habit for flush parking is the single best parking skill you can build. It uses the car's geometry the right way around: nose-out is much easier to exit safely into traffic than nose-in.
