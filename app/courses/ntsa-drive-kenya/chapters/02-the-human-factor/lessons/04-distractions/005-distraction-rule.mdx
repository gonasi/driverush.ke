Every distraction fits one of three types: your eyes leave the road, your hands leave the wheel, or your mind leaves the drive. Most distractions involve all three at once.

<Rule>
  A distraction is anything that takes your eyes, hands, or mind off the driving
  task. If it does any of the three, it does not happen while the car is moving.
</Rule>

<Callout type="tip">
  The simple cockpit-setup habit fixes most of it: phone in a mount, route
  loaded, snacks in reach, music chosen, mirrors and seat correct. Five seconds
  before you turn the key saves you from a five-second glance later.
</Callout>
