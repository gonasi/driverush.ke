Using a phone behind the wheel multiplies your crash risk by **four**. Texting pushes it past **eight**. That is not a slogan, it is the consistent finding from every major crash study done in the last decade.

The reason is brutal mechanics. Reading a single WhatsApp message takes about five seconds. At 60 km/h on Thika Road, that is 83 metres travelled with your eyes off the road. The length of a football pitch.

<Rule>
  Anything that pulls your eyes, hands, or attention away from driving is a
  distraction. In Kenya, using a handheld phone while driving is illegal and
  carries a fine and licence points.
</Rule>
