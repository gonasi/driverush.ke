Most drivers look at the road. Few **see** it.

Passive observation watches whatever happens to be in front of you. Active observation scans, predicts, and tests, eyes constantly moving between far, near, mirrors, and the blind spots.

<Rule>
  Move your eyes every two seconds: distance, near, left mirror, right mirror,
  centre mirror. A still gaze is a missing hazard.
</Rule>
