A shoulder check is a half-second glance, not a full turn. You're not looking for cars in your lane, your mirrors do that. You're looking for the boda boda, the cyclist, the runner hiding in the blind spot.

<Callout type="tip">
  Always shoulder-check before lane changes, joining a road, and pulling away
  from the kerb. The half-second never costs you. The crash from skipping it
  costs everything.
</Callout>
