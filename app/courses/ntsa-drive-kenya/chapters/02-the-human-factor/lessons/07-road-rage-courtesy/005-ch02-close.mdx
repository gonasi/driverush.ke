You have finished Chapter 2: The Human Factor. The hardest part of driving is not the gears, the roundabouts, or the parallel park. It is the seven things in this chapter: where your eyes go, how tired you are, what you drank, what is in your hand, what is on your belt, what is in your boot, and what is in your temper.

<Mnemonic>
  Eyes scan. Sleep first. Zero alcohol. Phone down. Belt always. Kit ready. Rage
  off.
</Mnemonic>

The driver who masters these seven beats the driver who only knows the rules. Chapter 3 turns inward, to the machine itself: dashboard, pedals, mirrors, and what every warning light is trying to tell you.
