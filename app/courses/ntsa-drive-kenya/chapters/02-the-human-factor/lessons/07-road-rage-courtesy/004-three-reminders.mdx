Three habits separate the driver who arrives safely every day from the one who escalates. Drill these into muscle memory.

<Rule>
  One: assume the other driver did not see you, did not mean it, or is having
  the worst day of their life. Granting that benefit costs you nothing and saves
  you everything.
</Rule>

<Rule>
  Two: never use your vehicle as a weapon or a teacher. The horn warns. The
  brake stops. The accelerator goes. None of them communicate punishment.
</Rule>

<Rule>
  Three: if you feel rage rising, drop back, drop a gear, and breathe out twice
  as slowly as you breathe in. Physical calm forces mental calm. Then drive on.
</Rule>

<Callout type="analogy">
  Treat every other road user the way you would treat someone in a long queue at
  the bank. You do not know their story. You only get a kinder day if you let
  small frustrations go.
</Callout>
