Stuck on Mombasa Road at 5pm. A matatu cuts in front of you with no indicator. Your hands tighten on the wheel. Your foot drifts to the accelerator. You feel the urge to teach this driver a lesson.

That is the road rage spiral. It starts as a flash of frustration and ends with someone in a hospital or a police cell. It is one of the leading human factors in Kenyan road incidents, and almost every driver believes it cannot happen to them.

<Rule>
  The other driver's bad behaviour is their problem. Your response is yours. A
  professional driver never lets another driver decide how they drive.
</Rule>
