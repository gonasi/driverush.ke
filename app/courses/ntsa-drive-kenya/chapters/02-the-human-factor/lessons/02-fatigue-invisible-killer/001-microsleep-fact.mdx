Two seconds. Eyes closed. That's a microsleep, and you won't even notice it happened.

At 100 km/h on the Mombasa highway, two seconds of closed eyes is 55 metres of car driven by nobody. The length of a swimming pool. The distance from the lay-by to the lorry you didn't see.

<Rule>
  Fatigue does not announce itself. By the time you yawn, your reaction time is
  already shot. Microsleeps happen before you know you're tired.
</Rule>

This lesson is short on purpose. Fatigue is the silent number on every accident report, and almost every driver underestimates it.
