Here is the comparison drivers never want to hear: **17 hours awake impairs you as much as the legal alcohol limit. 24 hours awake is worse than being drunk.**

<Callout type="warning">
  A driver who has not slept for a full day has the reaction speed of someone
  well over the Kenyan legal limit of 0.08 grams of alcohol per 100ml of blood.
  The road treats you the same way: as a danger.
</Callout>

Fatigue and alcohol attack the brain in the same places: judgement, reflexes, vision, attention. The difference is that the ALCOBLOW can catch the drunk driver. Nothing catches the tired one until the crash.

<Mnemonic>
  Tired is drunk without the breath test. Sleep is the only fix.
</Mnemonic>
