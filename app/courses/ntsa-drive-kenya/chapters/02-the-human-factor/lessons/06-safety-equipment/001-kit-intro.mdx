The day you really need your emergency kit is the worst day to discover something is missing or expired. By then, you are on the hard shoulder of the Mombasa highway in the dark and the spare tyre is flat.

A Kenyan road-ready boot carries six things at minimum: **two reflector triangles, a fire extinguisher, a first aid kit, a torch with working batteries, a spare wheel with the right tools, and a high-visibility vest.**

<Rule>
  The emergency kit is part of the vehicle. Owning it is not enough: it must be
  in date, in place, and you must know how to use each item before you need it.
</Rule>
