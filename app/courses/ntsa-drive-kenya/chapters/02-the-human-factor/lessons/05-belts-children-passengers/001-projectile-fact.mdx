In a 50 km/h crash, an unbelted 80kg adult hits the seat in front with about **4,000 Newtons of force**. That is roughly the weight of half a small saloon car landing on the person sitting in front of them.

Now picture that body is your child in the back seat. And the seat in front is your spouse. The unbelted passenger does not just kill themselves: they kill the people they crash into inside the cabin.

<Rule>
  Every occupant, every seat, every journey. The seatbelt rule has no
  exceptions: short trip, low speed, back seat, "just down the road." All
  exceptions are how people die.
</Rule>
