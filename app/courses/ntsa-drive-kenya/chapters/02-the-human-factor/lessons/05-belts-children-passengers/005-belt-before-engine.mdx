The cleanest way to make the seatbelt unforgettable is to chain it to a habit you already cannot skip: starting the engine.

<Mnemonic>Belt before key. Belt before key. Belt before key.</Mnemonic>

The driver checks: my belt on, every passenger belt on, child seats secured. Then the key turns. If you cannot say yes to every belt in the car, the engine stays off.

<Callout type="rule">
  In Kenya, the driver is legally responsible for ensuring every passenger wears
  a seatbelt. If a back-seat passenger is unbelted and you are stopped, you (the
  driver) pay the fine.
</Callout>
