The plan is the easy part. Make it before the first drink.

<Mnemonic>
  Three safe rides home: a designated driver, a taxi or ride-hail, or sleeping
  where you are. Pick one before you sit down at the bar.
</Mnemonic>

A short ride is cheaper than a tow truck. A taxi is cheaper than a hospital bill. A night on a friend's couch is cheaper than a funeral. The numbers are not close.

<Callout type="tip">
  Designated drivers exist for a reason. Rotate the role inside your group.
  Bolt, Uber, and Little Cab all run through the night in Nairobi, Mombasa,
  Kisumu, and most major towns. Save the numbers before you go out.
</Callout>
