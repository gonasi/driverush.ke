There is no quick way to reverse a high BAC. Black coffee does not work. A cold shower does not work. Time is the only treatment, and the liver burns alcohol at roughly one standard drink per hour, no faster.

<Callout type="warning">
  If you had three drinks at 10pm, you may still be over the legal limit at 7am
  the next morning. The breakfast commute crash is a real category in Kenyan
  road statistics.
</Callout>

<Rule>
  If you have drunk, you do not drive. Not "I feel okay." Not "it is only down
  the road." Zero exceptions, every time.
</Rule>
