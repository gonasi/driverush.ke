Alcohol is a **central nervous system depressant**. That is the technical name. In plain terms: it slows down every signal your brain sends to every part of your body.

The first signals to slow are judgement and inhibition. That is why a single drink makes a driver feel more confident, not less. The confidence is a lie. Reaction time, vision, and decision quality are already slipping while the driver feels fine.

<Rule>
  In Kenya, the legal limit is 0.08 grams of alcohol per 100ml of blood (or 35
  micrograms per 100ml of breath). But "legal" is not "safe." Even one drink
  measurably impairs driving.
</Rule>

The only fully safe blood alcohol level for driving is zero.
