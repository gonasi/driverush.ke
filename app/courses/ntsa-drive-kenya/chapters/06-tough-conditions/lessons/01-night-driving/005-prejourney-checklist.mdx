Night driving punishes anything you would get away with by day. Before you set off, walk around the car and check four things.

First, both headlights work, on low and on high, and the lenses are clean. A muddy lens cuts your beam by half. Wipe them with a damp cloth before any long night run.

Second, the windscreen is clean inside and out. An oily inside film turns every oncoming headlight into a starburst. Glass cleaner and a microfibre takes 60 seconds.

Third, the tail lights and brake lights work. At night a vehicle without rear lights is invisible to the traffic behind, and rear-end crashes are how most night highway fatalities happen.

Fourth, you are not tired. Night driving multiplies fatigue. If you have been awake more than 14 hours, find somewhere to sleep before you start.

<Mnemonic>
  Beams, glass, tail, you. Four checks before you turn the key after dark.
</Mnemonic>

<Callout type="tip">
  On the road, scan in this order: far down your low beam, the left kerb for
  pedestrians and animals, then the centre line. Stare at the centre line and
  you will drift into it.
</Callout>
