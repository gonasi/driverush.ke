Two cars, two techniques, one decision you make the moment you slide into the driver's seat: which one is this?

<Mnemonic>
  ABS: plant and steer. Non-ABS: pump and release. Wrong technique on the wrong
  car costs you the stop.
</Mnemonic>

If the ABS warning light is off and the car is a recent model, you are on plant-and-steer. If the warning light is on, or the vehicle is older with no ABS at all, you are on threshold pump-and-release. Treat them as different skills, because they are.
