Every Kenyan driving school teaches one mnemonic for the cockpit drill. It is hammered into you in the first week and you will hear it at the NTSA practical.

<Mnemonic>
  **P**etrol, **O**il, **W**ater, **D**oors, **E**lectrics, **R**ubber,
  **Y**ourself. PO WDERY. The full pre-start drill in one word.
</Mnemonic>

**Petrol**: enough fuel for the journey. **Oil**: dipstick between min and max. **Water**: coolant reservoir between the lines, plus screen wash. **Doors**: all closed and locked. **Electrics**: lights, indicators, wipers, horn all working. **Rubber**: all four tyres healthy. **Yourself**: seat, mirrors, belt, head clear, not tired, not in a hurry.

<Rule>
  Run PO WDERY before every journey longer than the supermarket run. It takes
  two minutes and it has saved more drivers than any airbag in history.
</Rule>
