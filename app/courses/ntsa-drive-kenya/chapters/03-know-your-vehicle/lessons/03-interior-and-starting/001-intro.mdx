You have walked around the car. Time to get in. But getting in does not mean turning the key. Before the engine fires, six things must be right inside the cabin: your seat, your mirrors, your seatbelt, the gear lever, the handbrake, and the dashboard.

<Callout type="analogy">
  Pilots have a pre-flight checklist they read every time, even after 30 years
  of flying. They do not skip it because they know it. They run it because
  routine is what keeps them alive. Treat your interior setup the same way.
</Callout>

A botched setup is how new drivers find themselves halfway down the road realising the mirror is angled at the sky, or the seat is too far forward to brake properly. Get it right while the car is still parked.
