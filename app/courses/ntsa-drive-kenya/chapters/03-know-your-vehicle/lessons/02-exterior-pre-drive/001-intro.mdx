The most expensive crashes in Kenya start before the key turns. A bald tyre. A blown indicator. A windscreen cracked across the driver's line of sight. None of them announce themselves once you are doing 80 km/h on Mombasa Road.

<Callout type="analogy">
  A pilot does a walkaround before every flight. Not because the plane is
  probably broken, but because if it is, the runway is the place to find out,
  not 30,000 feet up. Same logic for your car.
</Callout>

A proper pre-drive check takes two minutes. Two minutes against a lifetime of regret. The NTSA practical examiner watches for this, and a careful driver does it for every journey, not just exam day.

<Rule>
  Walk around the car. Eyes on every tyre, every light, the windscreen, and
  underneath. Do it before every long trip. Do it every morning.
</Rule>
