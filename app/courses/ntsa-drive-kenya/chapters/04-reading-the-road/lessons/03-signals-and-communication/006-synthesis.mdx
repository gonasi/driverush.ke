The road talks. Lights, hands, indicators, brake lights, even where another car is positioned: all of it is communication. Your job is to listen first and answer second.

<Rule>
  A police officer's signal overrides traffic lights. Traffic lights override
  signs. Signs override road markings. Markings override convention. In that
  order, no exceptions.
</Rule>

When the signals fail, you fall back on the next rule down. When everything fails, you stop and use your eyes.
