You now know the rhythm: Mirror, Signal, Manoeuvre. Three beats, in order, every time the car is about to do something different.

<Mnemonic>Look back. Tell them. Move.</Mnemonic>

In the practical test, examiners are watching the order more than the move itself. A perfect turn taken without checking mirrors is still a fail. A scrappy turn taken with full MSM is a pass.

Next: how the road talks back to you, through traffic lights, hand signals, and the officer at the junction when the lights are down.
