There is a phrase your examiner will say more than any other: <Term id="msm">Mirror, Signal, Manoeuvre</Term>. Three words, one habit.

<Rule>
  Every change of direction or speed begins with a mirror check, continues with
  a signal, and ends with the actual move. In that order. Always.
</Rule>

Get the order wrong and you fail the test, often without knowing why. Worse, on the road you surprise the driver behind you, which is how a clean turn becomes a rear-end shunt.

<Callout type="tip">
  MSM is not just for turns. It applies before changing lane, before slowing
  down, before overtaking, before pulling up at the kerb. Anywhere your car is
  about to do something different.
</Callout>
