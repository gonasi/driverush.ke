Three turns trip up most learners: the U-turn (180 degrees), the J-turn (a three-point turn in a narrow road), and the bend (a curve you take at speed).

<Rule>
  All speed adjustment and gear selection happens BEFORE the curve, never
  during. The bend itself is for steering only.
</Rule>

Brake on a straight line, gear down on a straight line, then steer. Try to do any of those things while the wheels are already turning and the car becomes unstable. This lesson drills the three turns and the one rule that saves all of them.
