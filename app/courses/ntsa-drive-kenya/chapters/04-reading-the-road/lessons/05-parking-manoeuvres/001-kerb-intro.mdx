Parking is where most drivers get caught looking inexperienced, or worse, get fined.

<Rule>
  A vehicle is correctly parked when it is parallel to the kerb, no more than
  half a metre from it, with wheels straight and the handbrake fully applied.
</Rule>

The technique is called <Term id="flush-parking">flush parking</Term>: the side of the car ends up flush with, and parallel to, the kerb. It takes practice, but it follows a fixed pattern. The pattern is below.
