Anyone can sit in a car. A driver makes it move smoothly and stop cleanly, every single time.

<Rule>
  Before you turn the key, the gearbox must be in neutral and your foot on the
  clutch (or brake, on an automatic). A car that starts in gear is a car that
  jumps forward into whatever is in front of it.
</Rule>

The rest of this lesson teaches the sequence: the cockpit drill that gets the engine on, the gear ladder that gets the car up to speed, and the controlled stop that gets it back to rest without drama.
