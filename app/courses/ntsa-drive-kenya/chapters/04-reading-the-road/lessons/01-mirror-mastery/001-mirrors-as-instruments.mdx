Most learners think mirrors are for parking. Wrong.

Mirrors are how you see the road behind you while keeping your eyes near the road ahead. They are instruments, like a pilot's altimeter, and you read them every few seconds whether anything has changed or not.

<Rule>
  In a moving car, your eyes belong on the road ahead and your mirrors. Nowhere
  else.
</Rule>

A driver who only uses mirrors before turning is a driver who gets surprised. A driver who scans them in rhythm with the road is a driver who is never surprised.

<Callout type="analogy">
  A good driver checks mirrors the way a chef tastes the soup: not because
  something is wrong, but to know exactly what the situation is at all times.
</Callout>
