The moment you put anyone or anything else in your vehicle, you carry a legal duty for them. A passenger trusts your seatbelts and your judgement. A cargo customer trusts your straps and your route. The law calls these the **conditions of carriage**, and they are different from ordinary private driving.

<Callout type="analogy">
  A private driver is responsible for themselves. A public service or commercial
  driver is responsible for every life and load behind them, by signature, by
  licence, and by Kenyan law.
</Callout>

This lesson covers the three things examiners ask about most: who can drive what, for how long, and with how many people on board.
