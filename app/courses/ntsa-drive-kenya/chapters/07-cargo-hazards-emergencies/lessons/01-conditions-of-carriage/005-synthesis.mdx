Three numbers worth memorising: **8** hours of driving in any **24**, **7** passengers maximum on a Category B licence, and **0** standing passengers in a long-distance PSV.

<Rule>
  Conditions of carriage are not paperwork. Each rule maps to a real failure
  mode: fatigue, overloading, and uncovered injuries when something goes wrong.
</Rule>

<Callout type="tip">
  In the NTSA theory exam, hours and passenger numbers come up almost every
  paper. Tie the 8-hour rule to fatigue, the 7-passenger rule to Category B, and
  the no-standing rule to long-distance PSVs.
</Callout>
