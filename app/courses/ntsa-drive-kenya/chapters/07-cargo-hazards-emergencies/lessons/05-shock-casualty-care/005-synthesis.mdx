Shock is what the body does when it is running out of working blood volume. Your job is to slow that process while help comes.

<Mnemonic>
  Lay them low, lift the legs, lock in warmth, leave the mouth empty. Four Ls.
</Mnemonic>

<Callout type="tip">
  Examiners pair shock questions with two traps: giving water, and giving
  paracetamol. Both are wrong. Always. The right action is comfort, warmth, and
  a clear airway.
</Callout>

You can now triage the scene, run Dr ABC, stop a bleed, place a recovery position, and treat for shock. Last piece: the paperwork side, because Kenyan law expects a report after every accident.
