When people say someone is "in shock", they usually mean shaken up. The medical term is something else entirely. **Medical shock** is a state where the body cannot get enough oxygenated blood to its organs. It is a quiet emergency, and it kills more crash casualties than the impact often does.

<Callout type="analogy">
  Imagine a hose with a sudden pressure drop. Water still leaves the tap, but it
  cannot reach the plants at the end of the garden. The plants wilt long before
  the tap runs dry. That is shock, and the "plants" are the brain, kidneys, and
  heart.
</Callout>

Shock can come from heavy bleeding, severe burns, crush injuries, or extreme dehydration. Treatment is the same.
