After every accident on a Kenyan road, the law expects two things: that you reported it correctly, and that you have evidence of what happened. Skipping either turns a recoverable claim into a personal disaster.

<Rule>
  Stop at the scene. Render assistance. Report the accident. Document the
  details. In that order, every time.
</Rule>

Driving off after a crash, even a minor one, is the offence of "failing to stop." Driving off after a crash with injuries is much worse. Insurance, criminal liability, and your licence all hinge on getting this right.
