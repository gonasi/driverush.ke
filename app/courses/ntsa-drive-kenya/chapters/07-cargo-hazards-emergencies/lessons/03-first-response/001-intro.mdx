A crash has happened in front of you. You are now a first responder, whether you wanted that job or not. The next three minutes decide a lot.

<Rule>
  Your priorities, in this order: your own safety, then warning others, then
  calling help, then assessing casualties. Do not skip a step to look brave.
</Rule>

Running into a scene before you know if a tanker is leaking, before traffic behind you has been warned, or before you have called 999 is how second crashes happen. Slow is smooth, smooth is safe.

<Callout type="analogy">
  Think of yourself as a triage nurse, not a hero. The nurse who steadies the
  room saves more lives than the one who lunges at the first patient.
</Callout>
