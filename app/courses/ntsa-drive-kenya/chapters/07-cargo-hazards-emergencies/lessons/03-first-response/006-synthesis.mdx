You have the scene playbook now: secure, warn, call, assess, stabilise. And you have the casualty playbook: Dr ABC, in that order, every time.

<Mnemonic>
  Scene: Safe, Signal, Send for help, See the casualty. Casualty: Danger,
  Response, Airway, Breathing, Circulation.
</Mnemonic>

<Callout type="tip">
  Examiners love the trap question: "the casualty asks for water." The right
  answer is always no. Same with paracetamol or any food. Empty stomach, calm
  casualty, wait for the professionals.
</Callout>
