A tanker on the Mombasa Road is not just a big lorry. Inside might be petrol, chlorine, ammonium nitrate, or radioactive isotopes. Each of those reacts differently to heat, friction, water, and impact. The international system that sorts them is called the **UN classification of dangerous goods**, and Kenya uses the same nine classes.

<Callout type="analogy">
  Treat hazmat classes the way a hospital treats blood types: getting the label
  wrong is not a small mistake, it is the kind that closes a section of the
  highway.
</Callout>

You do not need to memorise every chemical. You need to know the nine classes, recognise the symbol, and know which agency licenses what.
