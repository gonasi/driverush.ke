The adult human body holds about 5 litres of blood. Lose a litre and you start losing consciousness. Lose two and you are in serious shock. A first responder who can stop a bleed buys minutes that the ambulance turns into a life saved.

<Rule>
  Heavy bleeding is controlled by direct, firm, continuous pressure on the
  wound. Not tourniquets first, not herbs, not waiting for the paramedics:
  pressure, now.
</Rule>

<Callout type="analogy">
  Think of a punctured water pipe. You do not run for tools, you press your
  thumb on the hole. Same principle, same urgency.
</Callout>
