Why on their side, and not flat on their back? Because an unconscious person on their back is at the mercy of their own anatomy. The tongue is a muscle, and when it relaxes it can fall back and block the airway. Vomit and blood pool in the throat. The chest cannot expand fully against the floor of the ribcage.

Roll them onto their side and gravity becomes a friend instead of an enemy: the tongue falls away from the airway, fluids drain out of the mouth, and the chest can move.

<Rule>
  Unconscious and breathing, no spinal injury suspected: recovery position.
  Every time.
</Rule>

<Callout type="tip">
  Examiners ask this in two ways: "What position for an unconscious breathing
  casualty?" and "Why?" Know both. The position is recovery (on their side). The
  reason is to keep the airway clear and let fluids drain out.
</Callout>
