You can't skip this step: NTSA's [Provisional Driving Licence page](https://ntsa.go.ke/services/service/provisional-driving-licence-pdl) says a learner is required to enrol at a licensed driving school. This guide covers what schools charge, how to check that one is licensed, and what to ask before you pay.

## What driving schools in Kenya charge

These are prices that schools publish themselves. Our list is short on purpose: we only include a school when we can confirm its price from its own published page. We don't rank schools, because we have no data on which teach best.

So expect anything from around KSh 14,000 at these public institutions to KSh 25,000 or more for a regular course at a national brand. Prices change, and these may not cover everything, so confirm the total with the school.

AA Kenya says it has over 100 branches in major towns. For motorcycles, it lists A2 at KSh 5,780, and Kisumu National Polytechnic lists Class A at KSh 6,950 including NTSA charges.

**NTSA's fees are on top, unless a school's price includes them.** They come to [KES 5,500 for a first licence](/ntsa-test): KES 650 provisional, KES 1,050 test booking, KES 750 interim and KES 3,050 Smart DL. Kisumu National Polytechnic notes that students pay NTSA charges themselves through eCitizen, not to the school. Ask which of these your quote covers.

## How to check a driving school is licensed

- **Ask to see the licence.** NTSA's April 2026 notice says all schools must register, and that the licence must be printed and displayed on the premises. No licence on the wall is a reason to walk out.
- **Know the penalty is real.** The same report cites a fine of up to KSh 20,000, or imprisonment, for running a school without proper registration.
- **Ask NTSA if you're unsure.** NTSA says a list of licensed schools is available on its website, but we couldn't find it when we checked, so [contact NTSA](https://ntsa.go.ke/contact) directly.
- **Be careful with agents.** NTSA warned in September 2026 about fraudsters posing as driving school agents and promising to fast-track licences.

## Questions to ask before you pay

1. Can I see your NTSA licence for this branch?
2. What's the total, in writing, and what does it include?
3. Are NTSA's fees included, or do I pay them separately through eCitizen?
4. How many lessons do I get, and how much of that is me behind the wheel?
5. Which licence class is this course for, and is it automatic or manual?
6. Will you help me book the test, and can I practise on a Model Town Board?
7. What happens, and what does it cost, if I need a retest?

## How long does driving school take?

It depends on the school and the class. As one published example, Meru University of Science and Technology lists 25 practical and theory lessons over 1 month for Class B. Remember the timing rules too: your PDL has to mature for 21 days before you can book the test, and it's valid for 3 months.

## Make the most of your school

Your school teaches you to drive. What it can't do is memorise the road signs for you, and the [NTSA test](/ntsa-test) expects you to know them along with the [Model Town Board](/model-town-board). Use a few spare minutes between lessons on the <Link to="/road-signs/pelican">Pelican chart</Link> or with <Link to="/road-signs/3d">3D signs</Link>. Both are free, with no signup, and work alongside your school, not instead of it.
