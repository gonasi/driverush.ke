Looking for an alternative to Dereva Prep, or trying to choose between the two? Both help Kenyan learners prepare for the NTSA test, but they're good at different things. We run DriveRush, so weigh this with that in mind. We've tried to be fair, including about where Dereva Prep is ahead.

DriveRush isn't affiliated with Dereva Prep. Everything about Dereva Prep here comes from [its public pages](https://derevaprep.app), as they stood on 5 November 2026. It may have changed since, so check its site for the latest.

## DriveRush and Dereva Prep compared

|                          | DriveRush                                                          | Dereva Prep                                                                 |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Road signs**           | 83 signs on the Pelican chart, free                                | Identify-the-sign practice, 5 free                                          |
| **3D signs**             | Yes, spin 82 signs and read them from any angle                    | None that we saw                                                            |
| **Model Town Board**     | A written [guide](/model-town-board). No simulator yet             | A simulator with moves checked as you go and a route finder, 2 free games   |

| **Sign-up**              | None                                                               | None for the free tier. Sign in to save progress                            |
| **Price**                | Free, no signup                                                    | Free tier, then Premium through M-Pesa, with the price shown before you pay |

## When Dereva Prep is the better choice

If you want to practise the Model Town Board on a screen today, Dereva Prep has a simulator and we don't yet. It also has a much bigger bank of theory questions.

## When DriveRush is the better choice

If your priority is memorising road signs, that's the one thing we're built for. You can train on all 83 signs without paying or signing up, and you can spin them in 3D, the way you'll see them from different angles on the road.

- <Link to="/road-signs/pelican">Train on the Pelican chart</Link>
- <Link to="/road-signs/3d">Spin signs in 3D</Link>
- [See all the road signs and what they mean](/road-signs)

You don't have to pick just one. Plenty of learners use more than one tool, alongside the driving school that NTSA requires.
