Looking for an alternative to Dereva Prep, or trying to choose between the two? Both help Kenyan learners prepare for the NTSA test, but they're good at different things. We run DriveRush, so weigh this with that in mind. We've tried to be fair, including about where Dereva Prep is ahead.

DriveRush isn't affiliated with Dereva Prep. Everything about Dereva Prep here comes from [its public pages](https://derevaprep.app), as they stood on 10 November 2026. It may have changed since, so check its site for the latest.

## When Dereva Prep is the better choice

Dereva Prep has a much bigger bank of theory questions, and its Model Town Board simulator has a route finder, which ours does not.

## When DriveRush is the better choice

If you want to practise the Model Town Board without paying, our trainer is free: the board sits on a table, as it does at a driving school, and you drive a car on it. You can also train on all 83 road signs without paying or signing up, and spin them in 3D, the way you'll see them from different angles on the road.
