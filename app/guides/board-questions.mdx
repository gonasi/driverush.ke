These are the kinds of questions the Model Town Board is used to ask. Each answer comes from a rule in NTSA's learner handbook, and the rule is given with it. Say your answer out loud before you open one.

We don't have the questions any test centre uses, and NTSA does not publish them. Use these to check that you know the rules.

## Lanes

<Accordion className="my-6">
  <AccordionItem summary="How are the lanes numbered?" open>
    From the kerb inwards. Lane 1 is beside the kerb, and the highest number is
    beside the middle of the road.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem summary="You are on a four-lane road and want to turn left at the roundabout. Which lane?">
    Lane 1. It goes straight or turns left.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem summary="You want to go straight across the roundabout. Which lanes can you use?">
    Lanes 1, 2 or 3. Lane 2 goes straight only.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem summary="You want to turn right at the roundabout. Which lanes?">
    Lane 3 or lane 4. Lane 3 goes straight or turns right.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem summary="You want to go round the roundabout and come back down the road you came up. Which lane?">
    Lane 4, the lane beside the central reserve.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem summary="You are in lane 2 near the roundabout and realise you need to turn right. The lines beside you are continuous. What do you do?">
    Stay in lane 2 and go straight. A continuous white line means no changing
    lanes, and lane 2 goes straight only. Then find another correct route.
  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>

## The roundabout

<Accordion className="my-6">
  <AccordionItem summary="Which way do you go round the roundabout?">
    Clockwise. You keep left in Kenya.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem summary="Name the five things you must not do on the roundabout.">
    Stop, change lanes, park, overtake or wait.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem summary="You entered in the wrong lane. Can you move across once you are on the roundabout?">
    No. Changing lanes on the roundabout is not allowed. Choose your lane before
    you enter.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem summary="Which lane may go all the way round?">
    Only lane 4, the innermost lane. In the other lanes you take your exit the
    first time you reach it.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem summary="What are the three common mistakes at the roundabout?">
    Approaching in the wrong lane, leaving in the wrong lane, and changing lanes
    on the roundabout.
  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>

## Routes

<Accordion className="my-6">
  <AccordionItem summary="What are the three route rules?">
    Take the shortest correct route, avoiding the parking area. If that isn't
    possible, take the longest correct route, still avoiding the parking area.
    Use the parking area only as a last resort.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem summary="There are two correct routes to where you are going. Which one do you take?">
    The shorter one, as long as it avoids the parking area.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem summary="The only short route cuts through the parking area, but a longer route avoids it. Which do you take?">
    The longer route. The parking area is a last resort.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem summary="The shortest way goes against a one-way road. Can you use it?">
    No. It breaks a rule, so it is not a correct route, however short it is.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem summary="There is a Stop sign on your route, but no traffic and no painted line. What do you do?">
    Stop anyway, at or before the sign. The instruction applies whether or not
    anything is coming.
  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>

## Parking

<Accordion className="my-6">
  <AccordionItem summary="How do you go into and out of an angle parking bay?">
    Drive in forwards and reverse out.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem summary="Which angle bays do you fill first?">
    The ones at the far end.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem summary="How do you go into and out of a flush parking space?">
    Reverse in and drive out forwards.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem summary="When may you drive through the parking area on your way somewhere else?">
    Only when no correct route avoids it.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem summary="May you park on the roundabout?">
    No. Parking is one of the five things you must not do on it.
  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>

## Now drive it

Answering a question is one thing. Putting the car in the right lane is another. <Link to="/model-town-board">The Model Town Board trainer</Link> gives you a task, lets you drive it, and tells you which rule you broke. It is free, with no signup.

Your driving school is still the place to practise on a real board, because the test uses a real one.
