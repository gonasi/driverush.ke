The Model Town Board is a small town drawn on a table, with a toy car you move along its roads. The rules are the rules of the road, shrunk to fit. These are the ones NTSA's learner handbook gives for the board.

## The three route rules

The examiner names a place to go. There is usually more than one way to get there, and the handbook gives three rules for choosing:

1. **Take the shortest correct route,** avoiding the parking area.
2. **If that isn't possible, take the longest correct route,** still avoiding the parking area.
3. **Use the parking area only as a last resort.**

"Correct" is the word that matters. A short route that breaks a rule, such as going the wrong way down a one-way road, is not a correct route at all.

## Keep left, and know your lane number

You drive on the left in Kenya. Lanes are numbered from the kerb inwards, so lane 1 is beside the kerb and the highest number is beside the middle of the road.

## Continuous lines and broken lines

- **A broken white line** between lanes means you may change lanes.
- **A continuous white line** means you may not. On the board the lines turn continuous close to the roundabout, so choose your lane before you reach them.
- **A continuous yellow line** down the middle of a road means stay on your side of it.

## The roundabout

Go round clockwise, in the lane you chose. The handbook lists five things you must not do on it: stop, change lanes, park, overtake or wait. Only lane 4 may go all the way round. <Link to="/model-town-board/roundabout">Which lane for which turn</Link> has the full table.

## Kerbs and the central reserve

The handbook lists the yellow kerb among the board's features. Treat it as the edge of the road and keep the car off it. The central reserve keeps the two directions of a dual carriageway apart. A gap in it is where a car can turn back.

## Stop, give way and crossings

- At a <Link to="/road-signs/stop">Stop</Link> sign you stop, even with no line on the road and nothing coming.
- At a <Link to="/road-signs/give-way">Give way</Link> sign, traffic on the major road goes first.
- At a <Link to="/road-signs/pedestrian-crossing">pedestrian crossing</Link>, slow down and be ready to stop.

Obey every sign on your route, in order. You can learn all {SIGN_COUNT} of them on the <Link to="/road-signs/pelican">Pelican chart</Link> or with <Link to="/road-signs/3d">3D signs</Link>.

## Parking

The board has angle parking and flush parking, and the route rules tell you to keep out of the parking area unless there is no other way. <Link to="/model-town-board/parking">Parking on the Model Town Board</Link> covers both.

## What to ask your driving school

Boards are not all the same, and the handbook does not cover everything. Ask your instructor:

- to show you the board you will be tested on, and practise on it with you
- which lane a U-turn through the central reserve is made from
- who gives way at each junction on their board
- whether your test centre still follows the 2016 handbook
