DriveRush doesn't have accounts, so we don't ask for your name, email or phone number to use the trainers.

## What stays on your device

Your progress and settings, such as which signs you know and your light or dark choice, are saved in your own browser's storage. They don't need an account, and clearing your browser's data removes them.

## What we measure

We use analytics to see which pages get used and where things break:

- **PostHog** records page views, clicks, performance measurements and errors. It can also record sessions, meaning how someone moved through a page, so we can find problems. Text you type into fields is masked.
- **Google Analytics** records page views.

These tools use cookies or similar storage, and an anonymous identifier, to recognise a returning browser. They also process your IP address as part of normal web traffic. We use this data to improve DriveRush, and we don't sell it.

Your progress may be attached to that anonymous identifier, for example how many signs you've learned, so we can tell whether the trainers are working.

## Who else is involved

The site is hosted on Vercel, which handles web requests. PostHog and Google provide the analytics above. DriveRush shows no ads. These services may process data outside Kenya.

## Your choices

You can clear your browser's data at any time, block cookies, or use a tracker blocker, and the trainers will still work. To ask about any data we hold, or to ask us to delete it, contact us at <a href={`mailto:${SITE.contact.email}`}>{SITE.contact.email}</a>.
