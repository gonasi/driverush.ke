## 1. Give Way vs Stop

The inverted triangle is "Give Way". The octagon is "Stop". Different shapes, different actions. **Give Way** means yield to anything that has the right of way; if the road is clear, you don't have to come to a full stop. **Stop** means come to a complete stop, then proceed when it is safe.

## 2. Roundabout warning vs Roundabout direction

The **warning** sign is a triangle with curved arrows; it says a roundabout is coming. The **direction** sign is a blue circle with white arrows; it tells you which way to go round. In Kenya we drive on the left, so traffic circulates clockwise. Same arrows, different shape, different meaning. If you only memorise "arrows", you'll mix them up. We wrote a whole piece on [reading a Kenyan roundabout in the wild](/blogs/roundabouts-kenya-yield-signal-survive).

## 3. Pedestrian crossing ahead vs Pedestrians

The crossing-ahead sign warns of a marked crossing. The pedestrians sign warns that people on foot may be around. One tells you a crossing is coming. The other tells you to expect pedestrians in the area. Both mean slow down and look, but they are not the same sign.

## 4. Slippery road vs Loose chippings

The slippery-road warning is about a surface that has lost grip. The loose-chippings warning is about a road surface that can throw stones. Same situation (less grip or control), different cause. Slow down for either, but know which is which.

## 5. No overtaking vs End of no overtaking

One prohibits overtaking. The other cancels the prohibition. People remember the prohibition and forget the cancellation.
