Learning to drive costs two things: NTSA's fees, which are fixed, and a driving school's fee, which is not. This post sticks to figures we can source. DriveRush is not affiliated with the NTSA, so check the NTSA service pages linked below for current fees.

## The NTSA fees

Everything runs through [eCitizen](https://ecitizen.go.ke) and the NTSA service portal. The fees for one licence:

- **Provisional Driving Licence (PDL):** KES 650. Valid 3 months. ([NTSA](https://ntsa.go.ke/services/service/provisional-driving-licence-pdl))
- **Driver test booking:** KES 1,050, after your PDL has matured for 21 days. ([NTSA](https://ntsa.go.ke/services/service/driver-test-booking))
- **Interim Driving Licence:** KES 750, valid 6 months, which you print yourself after passing. ([NTSA](https://ntsa.go.ke/services/service/interim-driving-license-idl))
- **Smart Driving Licence:** KES 3,050, ready in 5 working days. ([NTSA](https://ntsa.go.ke/services/service/application-for-a-smart-driving-licence))

That adds up to **KES 5,500** in official fees. If you do not pass, you apply for a retest. NTSA's test booking page doesn't list a separate retest fee, so check the amount in the portal or with your school.

## The driving school

NTSA says the learner is required to enrol into a licensed driving school, so this cost is not optional. The fee varies a lot between schools and we have not surveyed the market, so we will not give you a typical price. Two published figures for reference:

- AA Kenya's own site lists first-time B1/B2 Regular at KSh 24,900 and Premier at KSh 50,000. Its A2 motorcycle course is KSh 5,780.
- Meru University of Science and Technology's driving school page (dated 2021, so likely out of date) lists Class B at KSh 14,850 all in.

Ask every school what its price includes: number of lessons, car hire on test day, and any extras. The school must be registered and licensed by NTSA and must display its licence on the premises. Our guide to [choosing a driving school](/driving-schools-kenya) has more.

## Costs to budget for on top

These depend on you, so we give no figures:

- **A retest.** A second test booking fee, and possibly more lessons first.
- **Extra lessons.** If your instructor says you are not ready, listen. It is cheaper than a failed test.
- **Transport to your test centre.** You choose the centre when you book, so pick one you can reach easily.
- **Printing.** You print your interim licence yourself.

## Where to save without sabotaging the test

**Learn the signs for free.** The free [Pelican chart](/road-signs/pelican) and [3D signs](/road-signs/3d) cover 83 signs, free, no signup. They support your school lessons, they do not replace them. See all the [road signs](/road-signs).

**Compare schools on what's included**, not on the headline price. A cheap package that leaves out test-day car hire is not cheap.

**Do not skimp on practice.** Booking the test before you can handle the manoeuvres costs more in retests than the extra lessons would have.
