An earlier version of this post asked whether you could skip a driving school. The answer is no. For the Provisional Driving Licence, NTSA says the learner is required to enrol into a licensed driving school. DriveRush is not affiliated with the NTSA, so see the [NTSA page for the PDL](https://ntsa.go.ke/services/service/provisional-driving-licence-pdl) for the current rule.

So the useful question is not whether to go to a school. It is how to get the most out of the one you pay for.

## What a school gives you

**A car with dual controls and an instructor.** A second brake pedal on the passenger side is the reason instructors can teach nervous beginners at all. You cannot replicate it in a relative's car.

**A neutral teacher.** An instructor has taught reversing and three-point turns many times. A relative may be learning to teach as you learn to drive. A school's repetitive, slightly bored teaching is what most learners need.

**A view on when you're ready.** Per Kenyans.co.ke (2 August 2025), the driving school judges your readiness and helps you book the test.

## Choosing a school

A driving school must be registered and licensed by NTSA, and must print and display its licence on the premises (Kenyans.co.ke, 14 April 2026, reporting an NTSA notice). Ask to see it before you pay. We have a longer guide to [choosing a driving school in Kenya](/driving-schools-kenya).

Prices differ a lot between schools. For reference, AA Kenya lists first-time B1/B2 Regular at KSh 24,900 and Premier at KSh 50,000 on its own site, and Meru University of Science and Technology's driving school page (dated 2021) lists Class B at KSh 14,850 all in. Ask any school what the price includes before you pay.

## What to do between lessons

Lessons are only part of learning. Between them:

- **Learn the road signs.** The [Pelican chart](/road-signs/pelican) trains recall under mild time pressure, and [3D sign recognition](/road-signs/3d) helps you read signs at an angle. Both are free, no signup, and cover 83 signs. They support your school, they do not replace it.
- **Read the Highway Code.** Per Kenyans.co.ke (2 August 2025), the test covers the Model Town Board and the Highway Code. Our [Model Town Board trainer](/model-town-board) lets you drive the board and explains its rules, and the [Model Town Board questions and answers](/model-town-board/questions) check that you know them.
- **Test yourself.** The [10-sign check on our road signs page](/road-signs#sign-check) takes a couple of minutes and shows what you don't know yet.
- **Ask your instructor questions.** If a sign, a junction or a manoeuvre confuses you, say so in the lesson, not on test day.

For what the test involves, see our [NTSA test guide](/ntsa-test).
