The Smart DL is the card-format driving licence. DriveRush is not affiliated with the NTSA, and fees and requirements for replacements, renewals and amendments change, so this post does not quote them. It tells you where to start in the four situations that send people to the NTSA in a panic.

All NTSA services run through [eCitizen](https://ecitizen.go.ke), then the NTSA service portal. The old TIMS system no longer exists, so ignore any advice that sends you there.

If you're applying for the first time (not replacing), the post you want is the [2026 NTSA walkthrough](/blogs/ntsa-driving-licence-kenya-2026). For reference, NTSA lists the first-time Smart DL application at KES 3,050 and 5 working days, collected at a Huduma centre or NTSA after an SMS ([NTSA](https://ntsa.go.ke/services/service/application-for-a-smart-driving-licence)).

## 1. The card is lost or stolen

1. **Report the loss to the police** and keep the record they give you. NTSA may ask for it.
2. **Log in to eCitizen,** open the NTSA service portal and look for the replacement service.
3. **Read the requirements and fee on the service page** before you pay.
4. **Pay inside eCitizen** and keep the confirmation.

Then wait for the SMS before you go to collect anything.

## 2. The card is expiring or expired

Don't wait until the last minute. Look for the renewal service in the NTSA service portal on eCitizen and read the current rules there, including what happens if your card has already expired. Don't drive on an expired card.

## 3. The card is damaged

A bent or cracked card usually means applying for a replacement. If only the chip seems damaged, ask at an NTSA office what they advise before you start an application. Bring the card and your ID.

## 4. The details on the card are wrong

This is rare but it happens: a name spelt wrong, a wrong date of birth, a wrong photo or class. Take the card and your ID to NTSA and ask how to correct it. Whether you pay may depend on whether the error was in your application or in the printing, so bring any receipts you have.

## After the new card

A fresh Smart DL is the right moment to check that the class on the card is the one you need. We covered that in [which licence class you need](/blogs/class-b-c-d-licence-kenya).

If it's been a while since you drove and you want to refresh the signs before you get back on the road, the [Pelican chart](/road-signs/pelican) and [3D signs](/road-signs/3d) cover 83 signs, free, no signup.

## When in doubt

The most useful habit with any bureaucracy: take screenshots at every step. Receipt screen, payment confirmation, SMS, status page. When something goes sideways, the person who has the timestamps has the easier conversation.

Drive safe. Renew on time.
