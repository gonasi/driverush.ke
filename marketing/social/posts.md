# DriveRush videos: what to post with each one

Fifteen vertical videos (1080x1920) for Gonasi's Instagram, Facebook and TikTok. They are made to look like a learner's own screen recording: the app fills the screen, captions are plain typed labels, cuts are hard, and there is no brand frame. Each one closes with a small `driverush.ke` label. The files are in `out/`, each with a cover picture of the same name. They are not in git.

## How to post

- **Keep the caption short and casual.** The video already carries the words. One line, then the hashtags.
- **Link.** Instagram and TikTok captions can't hold a clickable link, so put `driverush.ke` in the profile bio. On Facebook, paste the link into the post.
- **Sound.** Each video has the app's sounds and a quiet music bed. For more reach, add a trending sound in the app at low volume and keep the original audio on.
- **Order.** One a day or every other day, in the order below. It alternates the board and the signs and opens with the strongest hook.
- **Replies.** Videos 7, 10 and 11 ask for a comment. Answer the first ones quickly.
- **Be honest in replies.** These are posted by the team, so don't answer as if you were a learner who found the site.

Suggested order: 1, 7, 2, 10, 4, 9, 6, 11, 3, 8, 5, 12, 14, 13, 15.
